Why Do Moods Matter?

Our mood changes many times a day. Sometimes we feel happy and sometimes we feel low, and both are normal. But when a heavy mood stays for many days, it can turn into stress and affect our sleep, studies and health.

Noticing how we feel is the first step. After that, small habits like deep breathing, a walk, good music and healthy food can make a big difference.

🌸 Relax Menu

Simple things you can order any day to lift your mood.

🧘 Meditation 🏃 Exercise 🎵 Music 🍎 Food 🌬️ Breathing 🌙 Daily Habits
🧘

Meditation

  • Sit straight and close your eyes for 5 minutes.
  • Count your breaths from 1 to 10 and start again.
  • Use a free guided meditation app before sleeping.
🏃

Exercise

  • Walk for 20 to 30 minutes every day.
  • Do simple yoga poses like child pose and cat-cow.
  • Stretch your neck and shoulders after long study hours.
🎵

Music

  • Listen to soft instrumental or lo-fi music while studying.
  • Play your favourite old songs when you feel low.
  • Try nature sounds like rain or birds before sleeping.
🍎

Food

  • Drink enough water through the day.
  • Eat fruits, nuts and curd instead of too much junk food.
  • Have a cup of warm milk or herbal tea at night.

🌬️ 1-Minute Breathing Exercise

Breathe in when the circle grows. Breathe out when it shrinks.

  1. Sit comfortably and relax your shoulders.
  2. Breathe in slowly through your nose for 4 seconds.
  3. Hold your breath for 4 seconds.
  4. Breathe out slowly through your mouth for 6 seconds.
  5. Repeat this 6 times.

🌙 Daily Habits

Sleep for 7 to 8 hours. Take short breaks while studying. Talk to a friend or family member when you feel worried. Keep your phone away for some time before bed.

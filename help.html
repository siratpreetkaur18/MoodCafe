🤝 Need Help?

You do not have to handle everything alone.

Talk to Someone

Feeling very stressed, sad or worried for many days is a sign that you deserve support. Start with someone you trust, like a parent, friend, teacher or school counsellor. Just saying it out loud can make it lighter.

Helplines (India)

📞

Tele-MANAS

14416

Free mental health support, available 24 hours a day.

📞

KIRAN Helpline

1800-599-0019

Toll-free mental health helpline run by the Government of India.

🚨 In an emergency, or if you feel you might hurt yourself, call 112 right now or go to the nearest hospital, and tell someone near you.

Calm Down Right Now

  1. Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell and 1 you can taste.
  2. Breathe in for 4 seconds and out for 6 seconds, 6 times.
  3. Drink a glass of water and wash your face.
  4. Message or call one person you trust.
